## 1. Purpose

To set out what is expected of personnel when they use company information and technology, and to make sure everyone has the security knowledge their role requires.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and temporary staff, from pre-employment screening until after they leave.

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Human Resources Security
- Background checks shall be carried out before employment, in proportion to the role. Roles with access to Restricted data require enhanced screening.
- Employment contracts and contractor agreements shall include confidentiality and information security responsibilities.
- Security responsibilities that continue after employment ends, such as confidentiality, shall be communicated at exit.

### 3.2 Acceptable Use
- Company systems shall be used for business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not create risk.
- Personnel shall not:
  - install unapproved software
  - turn off security controls
  - share credentials
  - use unapproved cloud storage or AI tools for company data
- Remote work is only allowed on managed devices, using approved secure connectivity.
- Lost or stolen devices shall be reported immediately.

### 3.3 Awareness and Training

| Audience | Training | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| All personnel | Security awareness, phishing, data protection | At onboarding (within 30 days) and annually |
| All personnel | Phishing simulations | Monthly |
| Developers | Secure coding (OWASP Top 10) | Annually |
| Administrators | Privileged access and secure administration | Annually |
| Incident responders | Incident handling and forensics | Annually and through exercises |
| Executives | Cyber risk and crisis management | Annually |

- Training completion shall be tracked. Anyone who has not completed it within 30 days of the due date shall be reported to their manager, and their access may be restricted.
- Personnel who repeatedly fail phishing simulations shall receive targeted coaching.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| HR | Carries out screening, includes security terms in contracts, and runs the disciplinary process. |
| Security Awareness Lead | Runs the training and phishing programmes and reports metrics. |
| Managers | Make sure their teams complete training. |

## Compliance and Exceptions

Compliance with this policy is monitored through the controls listed in the Framework Mapping section, internal audit (ISO 27001 clause 9.2) and management review (clause 9.3). Breaches may lead to disciplinary action under the HR disciplinary process (Annex A 6.4).

Exceptions must be requested using the [Policy Exception Request](../templates/policy-exception-request.md), risk-assessed under [POL-10](POL-10-risk-management-policy.md), approved by the CISO and recorded in the risk register with an expiry date of no more than 12 months.
